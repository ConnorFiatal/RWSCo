Comparing Camera Recording Systems

There's no single "best" video management system — the right one depends on your alerting, retention, remote-viewing, and budget needs.

SystemGood ForNotes
UniFi Protect Homes & small-to-mid businesses already on Ubiquiti gear Simple app-based viewing, tight integration with UniFi network equipment, straightforward licensing.
DW Spectrum Growing businesses wanting a scalable, camera-agnostic VMS Flexible licensing and strong multi-site support as you add cameras and locations.
Wisenet WAVE Businesses wanting enterprise-grade features without enterprise complexity Solid analytics and failover options, camera-agnostic.
Milestone XProtect Essential+ Small sites wanting a free tier with room to grow Free for small sites, perpetual licensing up the product line — but Windows-only and heavier to run than most small setups need.
Blue Iris Budget-conscious residential and small business setups Affordable, highly configurable, runs on standard Windows hardware.
Frigate Tech-comfortable customers wanting local AI object detection Open-source, self-hosted, strong smart-detection with the right hardware.

Not sure which fits your property? Tell us how you want to be alerted, how long you need footage retained, how you want to view things remotely, and your budget — we'll recommend a system and give you a straight answer on cost.
