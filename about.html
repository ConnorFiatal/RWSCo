About Redwood Signal Co

Local, hands-on, and focused on getting your systems right the first time.

Redwood Signal Co is based in San Lorenzo Valley and run by Connor Fiatal, Lead Integrator. We install and support security cameras, network equipment, structured cabling, access control, and VoIP for homeowners and businesses across Santa Cruz County, San Mateo County, and Santa Clara County.

We're not tied to one brand. We'll walk you through the tradeoffs between systems like UniFi Protect, DW Spectrum, Wisenet WAVE, Milestone, Blue Iris, and Frigate, and recommend what actually fits your property, budget, and how you want to use it — then install, configure, and support it.

We also work on systems we didn't install. If your cameras, network, access control, or VoIP setup from any manufacturer needs a repair, upgrade, or someone to finally make it make sense, we can take it on.
